Delete your data

Flock Rush: Sheep Color Sort (com.craftuz.flockrush) · CraftUz

In short: Flock Rush has no accounts and keeps your progress only on your device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes everything the Game has stored. We (CraftUz) do not have a copy.

1. Delete game data on your device

Your level, stars, coins, hats, boosters and settings exist only on your phone. To delete them, do one of the following:

  • In the Game – open Settings → “Reset progress” and tap it twice.
  • Uninstall the Game – touch and hold the Flock Rush icon → Uninstall, or remove it in Google Play → Manage apps & device.
  • Clear the Game's data – open Android Settings → Apps → Flock Rush → Storage → Clear data (menu names may differ slightly between devices).

Deleted coins and progress cannot be recovered.

2. Delete data held by advertising and analytics partners

Google (AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase) may hold data linked to your device's advertising ID or to the Game's app-instance ID (see our Privacy Policy). We do not keep this data on our own servers, but you can:

  1. Reset or delete your advertising ID – on Android open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. Data linked to the old ID can then no longer be connected to your device.
  2. Clear the Game's data (above) – the Game gets a new app-instance ID, so new analytics records are not linked to the old ones.
  3. Ask us to delete analytics data – e-mail sardoormeliev@gmail.com with the subject “Flock Rush – data deletion”. We answer within 30 days, delete what can be linked to the details you give us and forward the request to Google.
  4. Use Google's own controls – Google My Ad Center.

3. What happens next

We do not keep player data on our own servers. Analytics data is deleted by Google automatically after the retention period (at most 14 months). We keep our e-mail correspondence with you only as long as needed to handle your request.

Ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish

Flock Rush: Sheep Color Sort (com.craftuz.flockrush) · CraftUz

Qisqacha: Flock Rush o‘yinida hisob (akkaunt) yo‘q, natijalaringiz esa faqat qurilmangizda saqlanadi. O‘yinni o‘chirib tashlash yoki uning ma’lumotlarini tozalash O‘yin saqlagan hamma narsani o‘chiradi. Bizda (CraftUz) ularning nusxasi yo‘q.

1. Qurilmangizdagi o‘yin ma’lumotlarini o‘chirish

Darajangiz, yulduzlar, tangalar, shlyapalar, kuchaytirgichlar va sozlamalar faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi. Ularni o‘chirish uchun quyidagilardan birini bajaring:

  • O‘yin ichida – Settings → «Reset progress» bandini oching va uni ikki marta bosing.
  • O‘yinni o‘chirib tashlang – Flock Rush belgisini bosib turing → O‘chirish yoki uni Google Play → Ilovalar va qurilmani boshqarish bo‘limida o‘chiring.
  • O‘yin ma’lumotlarini tozalang – Android qurilmangizda Sozlamalar → Ilovalar → Flock Rush → Xotira → Ma’lumotlarni tozalash bo‘limini oching (menyu nomlari qurilmaga qarab biroz farq qilishi mumkin).

O‘chirilgan tangalar va natijalarni qayta tiklab bo‘lmaydi.

2. Reklama va analitika hamkorlaridagi ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish

Google (AdMob va Google Analytics for Firebase) qurilmangizning reklama identifikatori yoki O‘yinning ilova nusxasi ID’siga bog‘liq ma’lumotlarni saqlashi mumkin (batafsil: Maxfiylik siyosati). Bu ma’lumotlar bizning serverlarimizda yo‘q, lekin siz quyidagilarni qilishingiz mumkin:

  1. Reklama identifikatorini yangilang yoki o‘chiring – Android qurilmangizda Sozlamalar → Google → Reklama (yoki Sozlamalar → Maxfiylik → Reklama) bo‘limini oching va reklama identifikatorini o‘chirish yoki yangilash bandini tanlang.
  2. O‘yin ma’lumotlarini tozalang (yuqorida) – O‘yin yangi ilova nusxasi ID’sini oladi va yangi analitika yozuvlari eskilariga bog‘lanmaydi.
  3. Bizdan analitika ma’lumotlarini o‘chirishni so‘rang – sardoormeliev@gmail.com manziliga «Flock Rush – data deletion» mavzusi bilan xat yozing. Biz 30 kun ichida javob beramiz, siz bergan ma’lumotlarga bog‘lash mumkin bo‘lgan yozuvlarni o‘chiramiz va so‘rovni Google’ga yetkazamiz.
  4. Google’ning o‘z vositalaridan foydalaning – Google My Ad Center.

3. Keyin nima bo‘ladi

Biz o‘yinchilar ma’lumotlarini o‘z serverlarimizda saqlamaymiz. Analitika ma’lumotlari saqlash muddati (ko‘pi bilan 14 oy) tugagach Google tomonidan avtomatik o‘chiriladi. Siz bilan yozishmalarni faqat so‘rovingizni hal qilish uchun zarur muddat saqlaymiz.
