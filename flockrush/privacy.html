Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CraftUz ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Flock Rush: Sheep Color Sort (package com.craftuz.flockrush, the "Game"), distributed for Android through Google Play. If you have any questions, contact us at sardoormeliev@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • You play without an account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, hats, boosters and settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads: full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, automatically collects some device data, such as the advertising ID, as described below.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase receives gameplay statistics (for example which levels are started, won or lost) so we can balance and improve the Game.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's Settings → “Privacy settings”.
  • The Game has no in-app purchases, no chat and no social features. It does not use your precise location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone or files.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Game data on your device

Your level, stars, coins, collected hats and boosters, daily-reward streak and settings (music, sound, vibration) are stored on your device only. We do not receive them. They are deleted when you use Settings → “Reset progress”, clear the Game's data or uninstall it.

2.2 Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Google Analytics for Firebase receives events such as: the Game started, a level started / completed / failed (level number, stars, mistakes, time), a booster used, a hat bought with coins, a rewarded video requested or watched. Firebase adds device information (phone model, Android version, language, country / region derived from the IP address) and an app-instance ID, a random identifier of this installation. The advertising ID is not collected for analytics. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics identifiers are used only if you agree in the consent form (Google's Consent Mode). Google keeps this data for a limited time (at most 14 months).

2.3 Information collected automatically by partners (SDKs)

When you play, the software development kits (SDKs) of our partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers – advertising ID (Google Advertising ID), app set ID, app-instance ID, IP address;
  • Approximate location – country or region derived from the IP address;
  • Device information – device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type;
  • App interactions – app launches, levels played, ad views and ad clicks;
  • Diagnostics – crash logs and performance data.

3. How the information is used

  • to show ads (between levels and rewarded videos), including personalised ads where you have given consent or where the law allows it, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad performance and to prevent ad fraud;
  • to understand how the Game is played (for example, which levels are too hard) and to improve it;
  • to find and fix crashes and performance problems.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform is shown on first launch. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the Game's Settings → “Privacy settings” (this item appears where the law requires it; elsewhere the same place opens this policy). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • US state privacy rights: residents of California and other US states can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising by deleting the advertising ID as above, through the privacy options in the Game's Settings where they are shown, or by contacting us.
  • Your game data: it is only on your device – Settings → “Reset progress” or uninstalling the Game deletes it.
  • Analytics data: we cannot connect analytics records to you because they carry no name or contact details. If you want them deleted, e-mail us; you can also clear the Game's data in Android settings, which gives your installation a new app-instance ID.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the Game are limited to content rated "PG". If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you reset it, clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game. Partners keep data for the periods stated in their own policies (Google Analytics: at most 14 months). Data sent by the Game and its SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

8. International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union, using legally recognised safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. This policy is available in English and Uzbek; if the two versions differ, the English version prevails.

10. Contact
