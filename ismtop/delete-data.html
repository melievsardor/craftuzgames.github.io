Delete your data

Ism Top: O‘zbek ismlari o‘yini (com.craftuz.ismtop) · CraftUz

In short: Ism Top has no accounts and keeps your progress only on your device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes everything the Game has stored. We (CraftUz) do not have a copy.

1. Delete game data on your device

Your progress, coins, found names, settings and name cards exist only on your phone. To delete them, do one of the following:

  • Uninstall the Game – touch and hold the Ism Top icon → Uninstall, or remove it in Google Play → Manage apps & device.
  • Clear the Game's data – open Android Settings → Apps → Ism Top → Storage → Clear data (menu names may differ slightly between devices).

Deleted coins and progress cannot be recovered. One-time purchases (for example, “Reklamasiz” – Remove Ads) stay linked to your Google account and can be restored later.

Card images that you saved to your gallery or shared in other apps are not part of the Game's data – delete them in your gallery or in those apps.

2. Delete data held by advertising and measurement partners

Our partners Google (AdMob) and Meta may hold data linked to your device's advertising ID (see our Privacy Policy). We do not have this data ourselves, but you can:

  1. Reset or delete your advertising ID – on Android open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. Data linked to the old ID can then no longer be connected to your device.
  2. Ask us to forward a deletion request – e-mail sardoormeliev@gmail.com with the subject “Ism Top – data deletion”. If possible, include your advertising ID (shown in Settings → Google → Ads). We answer within 30 days and forward your request to our partners.
  3. Use the partners' own controls – Google My Ad Center and the Meta Privacy Policy, which explains how to access and delete data held by Meta.

3. What happens next

We do not keep player data on our own servers, so there is nothing to delete on our side. We forward your request to our partners, who handle it under their own policies and timelines. We keep our e-mail correspondence with you only as long as needed to handle your request.

Ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish

Ism Top: O‘zbek ismlari o‘yini (com.craftuz.ismtop) · CraftUz

Qisqacha: Ism Top o‘yinida hisob (akkaunt) yo‘q, natijalaringiz esa faqat qurilmangizda saqlanadi. O‘yinni o‘chirib tashlash yoki uning ma’lumotlarini tozalash O‘yin saqlagan hamma narsani o‘chiradi. Bizda (CraftUz) ularning nusxasi yo‘q.

1. Qurilmangizdagi o‘yin ma’lumotlarini o‘chirish

Natijalaringiz, tangalar, topilgan ismlar, sozlamalar va ism kartalari faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi. Ularni o‘chirish uchun quyidagilardan birini bajaring:

  • O‘yinni o‘chirib tashlang – Ism Top belgisini bosib turing → O‘chirish yoki uni Google Play → Ilovalar va qurilmani boshqarish bo‘limida o‘chiring.
  • O‘yin ma’lumotlarini tozalang – Android qurilmangizda Sozlamalar → Ilovalar → Ism Top → Xotira → Ma’lumotlarni tozalash bo‘limini oching (menyu nomlari qurilmaga qarab biroz farq qilishi mumkin).

O‘chirilgan tangalar va natijalarni qayta tiklab bo‘lmaydi. Bir martalik xaridlar (masalan, «Reklamasiz») Google hisobingizga bog‘liqligicha qoladi va keyinroq tiklanishi mumkin.

Galereyaga saqlagan yoki boshqa ilovalarda ulashgan karta rasmlaringiz O‘yin ma’lumotlariga kirmaydi — ularni galereyadan yoki o‘sha ilovalardan o‘chiring.

2. Reklama va tahlil hamkorlaridagi ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish

Hamkorlarimiz — Google (AdMob) va Meta — qurilmangizning reklama identifikatoriga bog‘liq ma’lumotlarni saqlashi mumkin (batafsil: Maxfiylik siyosati). Bu ma’lumotlar bizda yo‘q, lekin siz quyidagilarni qilishingiz mumkin:

  1. Reklama identifikatorini yangilang yoki o‘chiring – Android qurilmangizda Sozlamalar → Google → Reklama (yoki Sozlamalar → Maxfiylik → Reklama) bo‘limini oching va reklama identifikatorini o‘chirish yoki yangilash bandini tanlang. Shundan so‘ng eski identifikatorga bog‘liq ma’lumotlarni qurilmangiz bilan bog‘lab bo‘lmaydi.
  2. Bizga so‘rov yuboring – sardoormeliev@gmail.com manziliga «Ism Top – data deletion» mavzusi bilan xat yozing. Iloji bo‘lsa, reklama identifikatoringizni ham ilova qiling (u Sozlamalar → Google → Reklama bo‘limida ko‘rsatilgan). Biz 30 kun ichida javob beramiz va so‘rovingizni hamkorlarimizga yetkazamiz.
  3. Hamkorlarning o‘z vositalaridan foydalaning – Google My Ad Center hamda Meta saqlayotgan ma’lumotlarni ko‘rish va o‘chirish tartibi yozilgan Meta maxfiylik siyosati.

3. So‘rovingizdan keyin

O‘yinchilar ma’lumotlari bizning serverlarimizda saqlanmaydi, shuning uchun biz tomonda o‘chiriladigan narsa yo‘q. So‘rovingizni hamkorlarimizga yetkazamiz, ular uni o‘z siyosatlari va muddatlariga muvofiq bajaradi. Siz bilan yozishmani faqat so‘rovni ko‘rib chiqish uchun kerakli muddat davomida saqlaymiz.
