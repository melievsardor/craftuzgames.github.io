Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CraftUz ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Ism Top: O‘zbek ismlari o‘yini (package com.craftuz.ismtop, the "Game"), distributed for Android through Google Play. If you have any questions, contact us at sardoormeliev@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account to play: there is no registration and no login. The Game does not ask for your e-mail, phone number, contacts or precise location.
  • CraftUz does not operate its own servers. We do not receive or store your personal data ourselves.
  • Your game progress, coins, found names, settings and the name cards you create are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases. Our advertising and measurement partners (Google and Meta) automatically collect some device data, such as the advertising ID, as described below.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's Settings → “Reklama roziligi (GDPR)”.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Information stored only on your device

  • Game data – completed levels and chapters, names you have found (including bonus names), coins and hints, your daily puzzle streak, achievements, unlocked decorations and a record of your purchases;
  • Settings – sound effects, music, vibration and reminders;
  • Name cards – the names and optional notes you type (for example, your child's name or a short wish) and the frames, colours and decorations you choose.

We never upload this data and we cannot see it.

2.2 Sharing and saving name cards

When you tap “Ulashish” (Share), the Game creates an image of your card on your device and passes it, through the Android share sheet, to the app you choose (for example, a messenger or a social network). When you tap “Saqlash” (Save), the image is saved to your device's gallery. This happens only when you take one of these actions. Once shared, the image is handled by the app you chose under its own privacy policy.

2.3 Reminders (local notifications)

The Game can remind you, for example, that your daily gift or the daily name puzzle is ready. These reminders are scheduled on your device with Unity Mobile Notifications; no push server is used and no data is sent to us. On Android 13 and later, Android asks for your permission before the Game can show notifications. You can turn reminders off at any time in the Game's Settings → “Eslatmalar” or in Android settings.

2.4 Information collected automatically by partners (SDKs)

The Game uses third-party software development kits (SDKs). When you play, these SDKs may automatically collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers – advertising ID (Google Advertising ID), app set ID, IP address;
  • Approximate location – country or region derived from the IP address;
  • Device information – device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type;
  • App interactions – app installs and launches, levels reached, ad views and ad clicks, ad revenue events;
  • Purchase history – in-app purchases made through Google Play (we never see your payment card details);
  • Diagnostics – crash logs and performance data.

3. How the information is used

  • to show ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded), including personalised ads where you have given consent or where the law allows it, to limit how often you see the same ad and to measure ad performance;
  • to process and restore in-app purchases;
  • to measure the performance of our own advertising campaigns (for example, how many players installed the Game after seeing our ad on Facebook or Instagram);
  • to understand how the Game is used and to improve it;
  • to detect and prevent fraud.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform is shown on first launch. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the Game's Settings → “Reklama roziligi (GDPR)” (this item appears where the law requires it). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Reminders: turn them off in the Game's Settings → “Eslatmalar” or in Android notification settings.
  • US state privacy rights: residents of California and other US states can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising by deleting the advertising ID as above, through the privacy options in the Game's Settings where they are shown, or by contacting us.
  • Access and deletion: because we do not keep your data on servers, uninstalling the Game or clearing its data removes everything the Game stored. To ask our partners to delete data linked to your advertising ID, e-mail us with the subject “Ism Top – data deletion” and we will forward your request, or contact the partner directly using the links above. Step-by-step instructions: Delete your data.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the Game are limited to content rated "PG". If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Card images you have shared or saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them. Partners keep data for the periods stated in their own policies. Data sent by the Game and its SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

8. International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using legally recognised safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. This policy is available in English and Uzbek; if the two versions differ, the English version prevails.

10. Contact
