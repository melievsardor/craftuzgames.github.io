Delete your account and data

Taxi Tashkent (com.craftuz.taxitashkent) · developer: CraftUz

In short: you can delete your Taxi Tashkent account and all its cloud data yourself in the Game, in a few seconds: HISOB (Account) → “Akkauntni o‘chirish” → “O‘CHIRISH”. If you no longer have the Game, send us an e-mail and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

1. Delete the account in the Game

  1. Open Taxi Tashkent and make sure your phone is online.
  2. Open the HISOB (Account) page – from the main menu (the account button at the top) or from Settings → HISOB.
  3. Tap “Akkauntni o‘chirish” (Delete account) and confirm with “O‘CHIRISH”.

The Game deletes your account immediately and starts again with a new, empty guest account. If your Google account was linked, it is unlinked as part of the deletion.

2. Request deletion by e-mail

If you cannot open the Game (for example, you uninstalled it or lost the phone), e-mail sardoormeliev@gmail.com with the subject “Taxi Tashkent – delete my account” and include one of the following so that we can find your account:

  • your player ID (shown on the HISOB page as “ID: …”), or
  • the Google account e-mail you linked in the Game, or
  • your driver name and the approximate date of your last game.

We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We complete the deletion within 30 days and reply when it is done.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

4. Data held by advertising and measurement partners

Google (AdMob) and Meta may hold data linked to your device's advertising ID (see our Privacy Policy). We do not have this data ourselves, but you can:

  1. Reset or delete your advertising ID – on Android open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID. Data linked to the old ID can then no longer be connected to your device.
  2. Use the partners' own controls – Google My Ad Center and the Meta Privacy Policy, which explains how to access and delete data held by Meta.
  3. Ask us to forward your request – mention it in your e-mail and we will forward it to our partners.

Hisob va ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish

Taxi Tashkent (com.craftuz.taxitashkent) · ishlab chiquvchi: CraftUz

Qisqacha: Taxi Tashkent hisobingizni va undagi barcha bulutdagi ma’lumotlarni O‘yinning o‘zida bir necha soniyada o‘chirishingiz mumkin: HISOB → «Akkauntni o‘chirish» → «O‘CHIRISH». Agar O‘yin endi telefoningizda bo‘lmasa, bizga xat yozing — 30 kun ichida o‘chirib beramiz.

1. Hisobni O‘yinda o‘chirish

  1. Taxi Tashkent’ni oching va telefoningiz internetga ulanganiga ishonch hosil qiling.
  2. HISOB sahifasini oching – bosh menyudagi (yuqoridagi hisob tugmasi) yoki Sozlamalar → HISOB orqali.
  3. «Akkauntni o‘chirish» tugmasini bosing va «O‘CHIRISH» bilan tasdiqlang.

O‘yin hisobingizni darhol o‘chiradi va yangi, bo‘sh mehmon hisobi bilan qaytadan boshlaydi. Google hisobingiz bog‘langan bo‘lsa, o‘chirish jarayonida u ham uziladi.

2. E-pochta orqali so‘rov

O‘yinni ocha olmasangiz (masalan, uni o‘chirib tashlagan yoki telefonni yo‘qotgan bo‘lsangiz), sardoormeliev@gmail.com manziliga «Taxi Tashkent – delete my account» mavzusi bilan xat yozing va hisobingizni topishimiz uchun quyidagilardan birini ko‘rsating:

  • o‘yinchi ID’ingiz (HISOB sahifasida «ID: …» deb ko‘rsatilgan), yoki
  • O‘yinda bog‘lagan Google hisobingiz e-pochtasi, yoki
  • haydovchingiz ismi va oxirgi marta o‘ynagan taxminiy sanangiz.

Hisob sizniki ekanini tasdiqlashingizni so‘rashimiz mumkin. O‘chirishni 30 kun ichida bajaramiz va tugagach javob yozamiz.

4. Reklama va tahlil hamkorlaridagi ma’lumotlar

Google (AdMob) va Meta qurilmangizning reklama identifikatoriga bog‘liq ma’lumotlarni saqlashi mumkin (batafsil: Maxfiylik siyosati). Bu ma’lumotlar bizda yo‘q, lekin siz:

  1. Reklama identifikatorini yangilang yoki o‘chiring – Android qurilmangizda Sozlamalar → Google → Reklama (yoki Sozlamalar → Maxfiylik → Reklama) bo‘limida Reklama identifikatorini o‘chirishni tanlang. Shundan so‘ng eski identifikatorga bog‘liq ma’lumotlarni qurilmangiz bilan bog‘lab bo‘lmaydi.
  2. Hamkorlarning o‘z vositalaridan foydalaning – Google My Ad Center hamda Meta saqlayotgan ma’lumotlarni ko‘rish va o‘chirish tartibi yozilgan Meta maxfiylik siyosati.
  3. So‘rovingizni hamkorlarga yetkazishimizni so‘rang – buni xatingizda yozing, biz hamkorlarimizga yetkazamiz.
